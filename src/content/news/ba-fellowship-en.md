I have been awarded a British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Sheffield for the project **“Mapping Air Pollution Health Inequalities: An Integrated Assessment Framework for Urban England.”**

The fellowship award is **£333,095.00**, with project reference **PFSS26\\260047**.

The research will focus initially on Yorkshire & Humber, integrating high-resolution air-quality mapping with socioeconomic, urban-form, vegetation and linked health data. It will develop new indicators across air quality, vulnerability and governance, with the aim of supporting targeted and equitable interventions.

[View the official British Academy award record](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/funding/schemes/postdoctoral-fellowships/past-awards/postdoctoral-fellowship-awards-2025-26/).
