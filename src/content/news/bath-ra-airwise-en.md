On 1 October 2025, I joined the **University of Bath** as a Research Associate on **“Air Quality Improvement and Resilience through Urban Morphology and Environment” (AIRWISE)**.

The project brought together research on urban morphology, environmental conditions, air quality and resilience. I collaborated with the Manila team to support comparative, place-based evidence and practical research exchange.

[Visit the AIRWISE project dashboard](https://airwise-dashboard.com/) to explore the project and its outputs.
